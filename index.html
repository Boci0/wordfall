About Wordfall
Wordfall for Windows
Version 1.9.0 · custom stages edition

Read. Commit. Survive.

Relay 12 is a night shift you cannot leave. The broadcast is coming apart one word at a time, and the picture is going with it. Patch the receiver, keep the signal alive, and hold the line until Dawn.

Boons are clean patches. Gambits overclock the transmitter. Hexes are corrupted packets that slipped through.

A one-screen typing game with a draft-only roguelite layer.
Keyboard required. No installer, no telemetry, no map, no branches.
The desktop build checks GitHub for updates; no account and nothing else is sent.
